Gear & Fuel Tips

  • Get fitted for running shoes at a running store and replace them every 300–500 miles.
  • Eat a familiar, carb-based snack 1–2 hours before long runs, like toast with peanut butter or a banana.
  • For runs over 75 minutes, practice taking an energy gel or chews every 30–45 minutes.
  • Nothing new on race day: no new shoes, outfit, or food.
  • Lay out your race kit the night before, including bib, pins, and watch.
